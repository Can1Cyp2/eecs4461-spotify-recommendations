The main alternative uses w=0.75: 25% established taste and 75% recent listening. The tables also include w=0, 0.25 and 1 from the five weights in my plan.

Each table shows ten songs selected from the 90-song catalogue. Songs used in either listening set are excluded from recommendations. The listening sets are hypothetical.

The scenarios compare the ten songs in recent listening with the ten in established taste:

  • Aligned: both sets contain the same ten songs.
  • Half different: five songs are shared and five are different.
  • Contrasting: no songs are shared, and genre and tempo differ.

The weight w controls how much recent listening contributes to the score. It is not the proportion of songs taken from that listening set. A higher similarity score means a closer match under my scoring rule, it does not show whether the listener would like the song. Scores are displayed to six decimal places; ranking uses the exact scores.
